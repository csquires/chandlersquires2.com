Concept extraction methods, including sparse autoencoders and transcoders, seek symbolic concepts within learned representations. This work studies their theoretical guarantees by treating concept extraction as a generative-model identification problem. A general identifiability theorem reduces the analysis to characterizing the intersection of two sets. Applying the framework to several common methods simplifies existing arguments and provides a foundation for designing concept extraction approaches with provable guarantees.
