Since September 2024, I have been a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Machine Learning Department at CMU, supervised by 
{{< myshortcode "https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~pradeepr/" >}}Pradeep Ravikumar.{{< /myshortcode >}}
Previously, I was a PhD Student at MIT, where I was advised by 
{{< myshortcode "https://www.carolineuhler.com" >}}Caroline Uhler{{< /myshortcode >}} 
and
{{< myshortcode "https://people.csail.mit.edu/dsontag/" >}}David Sontag.{{< /myshortcode >}}

My research focuses on theoretical and methodological foundations for **trustworthy, deployable AI**, informed and driven by some of the most challenging scientific and engineering applications, including drug discovery, manufacturing, and healthcare.
I am particularly interested in applications that present challenges for the current AI paradigm, e.g., high-stakes applications that require *safety*, *reliability*, and/or *transparency*, or data-scarce applications that require *extrapolation*, *generalization*, and/or *targeted data collection*.

To address these challenges, I study **structured/identifiable representation learning**, combining tools and perspectives from causality, neurosymbolic AI, and mechanistic interpretability, and **decision-theoretic learning**, combining tools and perspectives from statistical learning theory, debiased/orthogonal machine learning, and (targeted) Bayesian experimental design.
